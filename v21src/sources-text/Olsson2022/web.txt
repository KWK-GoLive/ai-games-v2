[Section "Induction Heads"] Induction heads are implemented by a circuit consisting of a pair of attention heads in different layers that work together to copy or complete patterns. [...] Specifically, the second head (which we call the "induction head") searches for a previous place in the sequence where the present token A occurred and attends to the next token (call it B), copying it and causing the model to be more likely to output B as the next token. That is, the two heads working together cause the sequence …[A][B]…[A] to be more likely to be completed with [B].
